## Learning Objective #1: Distinguish ASCVD and CVD

**Summary:**

- ASCVD (Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease) is used for lipid management guidelines
- ASCVD includes: Acute coronary syndrome, MI, stroke, symptomatic peripheral vascular disease (including aortic aneurysm)
- CVD (Cardiovascular Disease) is used for hypertension management guidelines
- CVD includes all ASCVD conditions PLUS heart failure, arrhythmia, and valve disease

### TABLE: ASCVD vs CVD

| Feature | ASCVD (for Lipid Mgmt) | CVD (for HTN Mgmt) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acute Coronary Syndrome | Yes | Yes |
| Myocardial Infarction | Yes | Yes |
| Stroke | Yes | Yes |
| Peripheral Vascular Disease | Yes (+ aortic aneurysm) | Yes |
| Heart Failure | No | Yes |
| Arrhythmia | No | Yes |
| Valve Disease | No | Yes |

> **Clinical Pearls & High-Yield Points:**

> When prescribing lipid therapy, use ASCVD terminology and risk assessment

> The danger of atherosclerotic plaque is NOT related to size, but to its tendency to rupture

> Plaque rupture leads to acute thrombotic occlusion causing MI or stroke

> Lipid lowering specifically targets atherosclerotic (plaque-forming) conditions

> **Memory Tricks & Mnemonics:**

> Remember "ASCVD = Arteries Stuffed with Cholesterol, Very Dangerous"

> CVD is the broader term - think "Complete Vascular Diseases" including heart function issues

## Learning Objective #2: LDL-C Goals

### TABLE: LDL-C Treatment Goals by Risk Category

| Risk Category | LDL-C Goal | Primary Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Very High Risk (ASCVD) | Less than 70 mg/dL | High-intensity statin |
| High Risk | Less than 100 mg/dL | Moderate-intensity statin |
| Moderate Risk | Less than 130 mg/dL | Lifestyle + consider statin |
| Low Risk | Less than 160 mg/dL | Lifestyle modifications |

> **Clinical Pearls:**

> The lower the LDL-C, the better for ASCVD prevention

> Every 40 mg/dL reduction in LDL-C reduces cardiovascular events by approximately 22%

> Consider adding ezetimibe or PCSK9 inhibitor if goals not met with maximally tolerated statin

## Learning Objective #3: Statin Intensity

### TABLE: Statin Intensity Comparison

| Intensity | LDL-C Reduction | Example Statins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High Intensity | 50% or more | Atorvastatin 40-80mg, Rosuvastatin 20-40mg |
| Moderate Intensity | 30-49% | Atorvastatin 10-20mg, Rosuvastatin 5-10mg, Simvastatin 20-40mg |
| Low Intensity | Less than 30% | Simvastatin 10mg, Pravastatin 10-20mg |

> **Memory Tricks & Mnemonics:**

> "A-R High Flyers" - Atorvastatin and Rosuvastatin are the high-intensity champions

> High intensity = high dose of potent statin OR moderate dose of super-potent statin
